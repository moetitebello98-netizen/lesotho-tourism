Travel tip: Confirm current prices, access, opening times, weather and road conditions with official or local sources before you travel.
Mountain adventure

Afriski Mountain Resort

Afriski is a mountain resort in the Maluti Mountains known for winter snow activities and scenic highland surroundings. Activities and conditions vary by season.

  • Possible interest: snow activities in winter, mountain views and relaxation.
  • Plan ahead: check seasonal operations, equipment requirements and accommodation.
  • Visitor reminder: prepare for cold weather and changing mountain conditions.
Natural attraction

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls is a famous waterfall near Semonkong. The water drops into a deep gorge, creating a striking view for visitors and photographers.

  • Possible interest: sightseeing, photography and guided outdoor activities.
  • Plan ahead: check road access and local guide availability.
  • Safety: stay behind barriers and follow local instructions near the gorge.
Highland scenery

Thabana Li'Mele

This website includes Thabana Li'Mele as a destination for further tourism research. Before publishing detailed visitor advice, confirm the exact location, route, local access and attractions from a reliable local source.

  • Research the route and transport options.
  • Check whether a local guide or permission is needed.
  • Prepare suitable clothing and supplies for highland weather.
